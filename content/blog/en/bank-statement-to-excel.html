Need to total your expenses for an accountant, build a budget, or find every charge from a particular subscription? When transactions are stuck in a PDF, every calculation means typing by hand. Converting to Excel turns it into a few minutes of sorting and filtering.

Where to get the PDF

Bank and credit card websites and apps usually let you generate a statement for a period as a PDF. Check whether there's also an option to export straight to Excel. If there is, use it. If you only have a PDF, or you received the file from someone else, converting is the way.

Important: use a PDF downloaded from the website, not a scan or a screenshot. A downloaded file contains real text, so tables are extracted accurately. A scan contains only an image.

How to convert

  1. If the file is password-protected, remove the password first with Unlock PDF.
  2. Open PDF to Excel and upload the file.
  3. Download the Excel file. Each table will be on its own sheet, named after its page.

Tidying up in Excel

A few quick steps turn the result into a table you can work with:

  • Combine sheets: a multi-page statement arrives as one sheet per page. Copy them all into one sheet, one under the other, and delete repeated header rows.
  • Amounts as numbers: if amounts are left-aligned or Excel won't sum them, they came in as text. Select the column, remove currency symbols and spaces with Find and Replace, and convert to numbers.
  • Dates: make sure Excel recognizes them as dates, not text, or sorting by date won't work.
  • Debit and credit: if they're in separate columns, add a balance or net amount column.

What you can do with it

  • Filter: every charge from a specific store, subscription or direct debit.
  • Pivot table: total spending by merchant or by month.
  • Spot duplicate charges or subscriptions you forgot you're paying for.
  • Prepare material for an accountant or a tax refund.

Security

A bank statement is one of the most sensitive documents you have. When you're done, delete extra copies from your downloads folder. If you're sending the file to someone, consider hiding irrelevant transactions, with help from the guide to hiding personal details.
