You've been sent an agreement, a sign-up form or a consent letter to sign as a PDF. There's no need to print it, sign with a pen, scan it and send it back. You can sign it on your computer in about a minute, Hebrew documents included. Here's how, and what to check before sending.

How to sign a PDF on a computer

  1. Open Sign PDF and choose the file.
  2. Create a signature. Either draw it in the box with a mouse or touchpad, or type your full name and let the tool turn it into a styled signature. Hebrew names are written right to left.
  3. Pick the page. Most contracts are signed on the last page, so that's the default. You can choose the first page instead.
  4. Pick the position: bottom right, centre or left. In Hebrew documents the signature line is usually on the right.
  5. Decide on a date. The tool can add today's date under the signature, as you would next to a signature on paper.
  6. Download the signed file and send it.

Draw or type?

A drawn signature looks closest to your signature on paper and is the natural choice for contracts. A laptop touchpad can make that awkward; if it comes out shaky, open the same page on your phone and draw with your finger. There's a separate guide for that. A typed signature suits simple forms and confirmations, where what matters is the signer's name.

Before you send: three checks

  • Open the signed file and zoom into the signature. Make sure it doesn't cover text or an important line. If it does, sign again in another position.
  • Check it's the right page. Some contracts have an appendix after the signature page, so the signature belongs on the second-to-last page. In that case take the signature page out with Split PDF, sign it, and put the file back together with Merge PDF.
  • Keep a copy. Save the signed version after sending; files on our server are deleted after an hour.

What the tool doesn't do

It adds one visible signature, on the first or the last page. It doesn't fill form fields such as name or address, and it doesn't add initials on every page. To sign a page in the middle of a document, split it out, sign it and merge it back, as described above. A password-protected document needs to be opened first with Unlock PDF.

Is a signature like this valid?

A visible signature on a PDF is accepted for most everyday documents: rental agreements, school forms, work confirmations and quotes. Some organisations and documents require a certified electronic signature with a digital certificate, for example some filings with authorities. The differences are explained in scanned signature, PDF signature or certified signature. For a contract with serious legal weight, ask whoever requested it which kind of signature they need.
