When you need JPG to PDF

Many forms only accept PDF files, but the documents you have are photos on your phone: a picture of your ID, receipts for an expense claim, pages of a signed form. Converting them into a single PDF gives you one tidy file instead of a stack of images, and it's what most offices and upload forms expect.

Scanning with your phone

You don't need a scanner. For a result that looks like a real scan:

  • Put the page on a dark, flat surface so the edges stand out.
  • Shoot straight from above, in daylight or a well-lit room, without flash.
  • Crop each photo to the edges of the page before uploading. Most phone gallery apps can do this.
  • Choose A4 as the page size so the PDF prints and displays like a normal document.

Several documents in one file

Add all the images at once, then use Move up to put them in order. For example, the front and back of an ID card, followed by a payslip. If you already have some pages as PDFs, create the PDF from your images here and then combine everything with Merge PDF.

Keeping the file small

Modern phone photos are large, so a PDF made from ten of them can exceed upload limits. Run the result through Compress PDF. Need the text to be searchable or copyable? Use OCR on the finished PDF.
