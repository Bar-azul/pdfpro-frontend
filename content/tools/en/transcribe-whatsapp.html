How to save a WhatsApp voice message

This is the only slightly confusing part, and each device does it differently:

On Android

  1. In the chat, long-press the voice message.
  2. In the three-dot menu (⋮) choose Share, and save it to Files or Google Drive.
  3. Pick the saved file here.

Another way: voice messages you've played are already on the phone. On most recent devices they're in Android/media/com.whatsapp/WhatsApp/Media/WhatsApp Voice Notes, split into folders by week. The latest message is usually the newest file in the latest folder.

On iPhone

  1. Long-press the voice message and choose Forward.
  2. Tap the share icon in the corner and choose Save to Files.
  3. Pick the file here from the Files app.

On a computer (WhatsApp Web or the desktop app)

Hover over the message, open the small arrow in the corner and choose Download. The file goes to your Downloads folder.

When it's useful

  • You're in a meeting, on a bus or next to a sleeping baby and can't listen.
  • Long messages with details: an address, a time, a shopping list or instructions are easy to find in text without replaying the whole message.
  • Someone at home who is hard of hearing can read messages instead of listening to them.
  • Keeping a record: a message from a landlord, a contractor or a client, saved as text with a date so it's easy to find later.

A message in another language?

Choose the spoken language, for example Arabic or Russian, and you get the text in that language. To read it in another language, save the transcript as a Word document, turn it into a PDF with Word to PDF and translate it with Translate PDF.

Long recordings

A call, lecture or meeting recording of up to 60 minutes goes through Transcribe audio, which can also give you video subtitles (SRT).
