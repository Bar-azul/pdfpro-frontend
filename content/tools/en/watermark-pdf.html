When to add a watermark

A watermark tells anyone who sees the document what it is and what it's for. Common uses:

  • Draft on a contract or proposal that isn't final yet.
  • Confidential on internal documents.
  • Copy of an ID for a specific purpose, for example "For the rental of 5 Herzl St. only". If the copy leaks, it can't easily be reused for anything else. This is one of the simplest ways to reduce the risk of identity misuse.
  • Sample on designs, photos or reports you're showing before payment.

Choosing the strength

Light keeps the document comfortable to read and suits drafts. Strong suits ID copies and samples, where the goal is to make reuse obvious.

How protective is it?

The watermark is drawn into every page, so it can't be switched off in a normal PDF reader. Someone with editing software and some patience could remove it, so it deters misuse rather than preventing it. To stop people from opening the file at all, add a password with Password protect PDF. To remove specific details completely, use Redact PDF.
