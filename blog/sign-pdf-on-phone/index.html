How to Sign a PDF on Android or iPhone (No Printing, No App)

October 9, 2026

Got a form to sign on WhatsApp or by email, and there's no printer around? You can sign the PDF right on your phone with your finger, without installing an app and without printing and scanning. The whole thing takes about a minute. Below you'll also find fixes for common problems, what to do when two people need to sign, and other ways to sign on a phone.

Step 1: Save the PDF on your phone

The file needs to be saved on the device so you can pick it:

  • iPhone: open the file in WhatsApp or Mail, tap the share button and choose Save to Files.
  • Android: when you pick a file from the browser, recent files, including ones from WhatsApp and Gmail, usually show up. If yours isn't there, see the next section.

On Android: where is the file you received?

Android has no single place where every file lands, which is the most confusing part. By source:

  • Gmail: tap the download icon (the down arrow) next to the attachment. The file goes to Downloads.
  • WhatsApp: a document you opened in a chat is already on the phone. On most recent devices it's in Android/media/com.whatsapp/WhatsApp/Media/WhatsApp Documents. The easier way: in the file picker, tap search and type part of the file name.
  • A website or a company portal: what you downloaded in Chrome is in Downloads, also reachable from Chrome's three-dot menu → Downloads.

This works in any up-to-date browser: Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox and others. No app needed.

Step 2: Sign it

  1. Open Sign PDF in your phone's browser and choose the file.
  2. Draw your signature with your finger in the box, or switch to Type and enter your name. If the signature comes out wobbly, tap Clear and try again.
  3. Choose the page, first or last, and the position: bottom right, bottom left or bottom center.
  4. Choose whether to add today's date under the signature.
  5. Tap the button and download the signed file.

Tip: if it's hard to draw a neat signature with your finger, turn the phone sideways. The box gets wider and you have more room to move.

Step 3: Send it back

The signed file is saved to your Downloads folder (on iPhone: the Files app → Downloads). Attach it to WhatsApp or an email like any other file. Open it once before sending to check the signature is where it should be.

What if the signature line is in the middle of the form?

The tool adds the signature at the bottom of the first or last page, which is where the signature line sits on most contracts and forms. If yours needs a signature somewhere else, such as a page in the middle, take that page out with Split PDF, sign it, and put the file back together with Merge PDF.

Is a signature like this valid?

For most everyday documents, like forms, consent letters, agreements between private people and rental contracts, a signature added to a PDF is accepted just like one signed on paper and scanned. Some documents require a certified digital signature with a certificate, mainly for government bodies and large transactions. The guide to signature types explains the difference and how to tell what you need. For a lease, see also how to sign a rental contract without printing it.

Two people need to sign

For example a tenant and a landlord, or two people on the same form. Sign the same file in turn:

  1. The first person signs, picks a position such as bottom right, and sends the signed file.
  2. The second person uploads the already-signed file, signs, and picks a different position such as bottom left.

Both signatures end up on the same page without overlapping, each with the date it was signed.

Common problems on a phone

  • The file doesn't appear when choosing a file. It was probably only opened for viewing, not saved. Save it as described above, or search by name in the picker.
  • The page scrolls when you try to draw. Start the stroke inside the box, not on its edge. Turning the phone sideways gives you a bigger box.
  • The signature came out small or crooked. Draw it large, across the whole box. It's scaled down automatically to a normal signature size on the page.
  • The file asks for a password. Remove it first with Unlock PDF (you need to know the password), then sign.
  • I downloaded it and can't find the signed file. It's in Downloads, usually with the original name plus a short suffix. In Chrome: three-dot menu → Downloads.

Other ways to sign on a phone

There are other options, each with its own strengths:

  • On iPhone, with no website at all: open the PDF in the Files app, tap the Markup icon (the pen), then + → Add Signature. Good when you need to sign at an exact spot in the middle of a page.
  • Adobe Acrobat Reader: for Android and iPhone, with a "Fill & Sign" option. You need to install the app, and some features require an account.
  • Samsung Notes: on some Samsung phones you can import a PDF into the app, write on it, and save it as a PDF again.

The advantage of Sign PDF here: it works the same way on any phone and on a computer, with nothing to install and no account, and adds the date under the signature with one tap.

Privacy

Your signature and the file are deleted from the server automatically after one hour and aren't stored anywhere else. There's no sign-up and no account is created.
