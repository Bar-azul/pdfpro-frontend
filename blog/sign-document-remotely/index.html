How to Get a Document Signed Remotely for Free

November 23, 2026

You need a signature from someone who isn't with you: a client, a landlord, a parent or a business partner. You don't need a subscription to a signing platform. Here's how to get a document signed remotely for free, and what to watch out for.

The simple way: send a PDF, get a signed PDF back

  1. Prepare the document as a PDF. If it's in Word, convert it with Word to PDF so the other side can't change the wording by accident.
  2. Sign first yourself if both signatures are needed, with Sign PDF.
  3. Send the file by email or WhatsApp, with a link to the signing tool.
  4. The other person signs on their phone: they open the link, upload the file, sign with a finger and download it. No app, no account.
  5. Get the signed file back and keep a copy.

A message you can copy: "The document is attached. You can sign it on your phone at pdfproapp.com/sign-pdf and send it back to me. Thanks!"

Tip: a ready-made signature

Sign a lot of documents? Make an image of your signature once with Create Signature, keep it on your phone and upload it each time instead of drawing again.

Android tip: share straight from WhatsApp

In Chrome on Android you can install PDFPro as an app: open the site and choose "Install app" from the menu. Then, when a PDF arrives on WhatsApp, tap Share, choose PDFPro and pick Sign. The file opens straight in the tool.

Several people signing one document

The file passes from one person to the next: each signs the version that already has the earlier signatures and sends it on. Agree in advance who sends the final version to everyone, so several versions don't end up going around.

When a signing platform or certified signature is worth it

  • High-value contracts or clients you don't know: a signing platform records who signed, when and from which device. That record helps if there's a dispute.
  • Many documents a month: templates and reminders save time.
  • When a certified electronic signature is required: then you need a certified ("green") signature, and a drawn one won't do.

After signing

  • Open the returned file and check the signature is in the right place and the wording hasn't changed.
  • Keep the email or WhatsApp thread. It's part of the record that the document was sent and signed.
  • Document with personal details? Lock it with a PDF password before sending it to a third party.
