What Is a "Green Signature" in Israel, and Do You Need One?

November 23, 2026

Someone in Israel asked you for a "green signature" (חתימה ירוקה), or you saw the term and aren't sure it applies to you. Here's what it is, who actually needs one, and when an ordinary signature on a PDF is enough.

What a green signature is

"Green signature" is the informal Israeli name for a certified electronic signature (חתימה אלקטרונית מאושרת), the highest level in Israel's Electronic Signature Law of 2001. It relies on a personal digital certificate issued by a certification authority, a registered and supervised company, after it has identified the signer. The certificate usually sits on a smart card or a USB token, and some providers offer other options too.

Where the name comes from isn't official. Some trace it to the green mark Adobe Acrobat shows when a signature is valid, others to the paper it saves.

How it differs from an ordinary signature

  • Identity: a drawn or typed signature doesn't technically prove who signed. A green signature is tied to a person who was identified in advance.
  • Integrity: the signature is bound mathematically to the file's content. Any change after signing, even a comma, shows up when the signature is checked.
  • Cost and effort: a drawn signature is free and takes a minute. A green signature needs issuing, identification and usually a paid provider.

Who needs one

Mostly professionals filing documents in the systems of government bodies and courts, such as lawyers, accountants and tax advisers, and organisations whose regulator requires it. If you need one, you'll usually be told explicitly: "a certified electronic signature is required" or "sign with a smart card".

When an ordinary PDF signature is enough

Most everyday documents only ask you to "sign and send":

  • A lease between tenant and landlord.
  • A school, kindergarten or after-school form.
  • A receipt confirmation, a quote or an onboarding form.
  • An agreement with a client or a freelancer.

There, a visible drawn or typed signature is generally accepted as long as both sides agree to work that way and keep a copy. Do it with Sign PDF without printing, and use Create Signature to make an image of your signature once and reuse it. For a large contract or when in doubt, ask the other side or a lawyer.

How to check whether a document has a green signature

  1. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free).
  2. If it has a digital signature, a bar at the top or a "Signatures" panel shows the signer's details.
  3. A valid mark means the signature checks out and the file hasn't changed since. A warning means the certificate isn't recognised or the file was changed.

A signature that looks like a signature but doesn't appear in the panel is an ordinary visible signature, however nicely it's styled.

A common mistake: editing a signed file

If you received a PDF with a green signature, don't compress, merge or rotate it before passing it on. Any tool that rewrites the file, including compression and merging, breaks the check and the signature shows as invalid. Need to send it with other documents? Attach it as a separate file.

In short

A green signature is a certified electronic signature with a personal certificate. You need it when it's explicitly required, mainly in government and court systems. For everything else, an ordinary PDF signature is enough. The guide to signature types compares all the options.

This guide is general information, not legal advice.
