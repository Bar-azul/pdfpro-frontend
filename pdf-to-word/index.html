PDF to Word converter

Turn a PDF into an editable Word document. Layout, tables and images are kept, and Hebrew or Arabic text stays in the right order.

    
    
    
    
    Files are processed on our server and deleted automatically after one hour.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    When you need PDF to Word
    

    A PDF is built for reading, not for editing. Converting to Word is the quickest way to update an old contract, reuse text from a report, fix a CV you only have as a PDF, or fill in a form that wasn't made fillable.
    

    Why Hebrew PDFs often break in other converters
    

    A PDF doesn't store sentences. It stores the position of each character on the page. A converter has to rebuild the reading order from those positions, and many were built for left-to-right text only. The result is Hebrew words spelled backwards, numbers flipped inside sentences, and mixed Hebrew-English paragraphs scrambled. PDFPro detects right-to-left text when rebuilding the document. The RTL guide explains the problem in detail.
    

    Getting the best result
    

      

    • Start from the original PDF, not a PDF that was printed and scanned. A scan is an image, and needs OCR instead.
      • 

    • Check tables after converting. Simple tables come through cleanly. Tables with merged cells may need a quick fix.
      • 

    • Expect small layout shifts in designed documents like brochures, where text sits in many separate boxes.
      • 

    

    Going the other way
    

    Finished editing? Turn the document back into a PDF with Word to PDF.

    Frequently asked questions
    
    
    Is it free?
    Yes. There's no sign-up and no watermark. Each tool has an hourly limit to keep the service available for everyone.
    Will the formatting be kept?
    Text, fonts, tables and images are kept as closely as possible. Very complex layouts, like magazines with overlapping elements, may need small fixes afterwards.
    Does it work with Hebrew PDFs?
    Yes. Right-to-left text is detected so Hebrew words and sentences don't come out reversed. Mixed Hebrew and English paragraphs keep their order.
    Can I convert a scanned PDF?
    A scanned PDF is an image of text, so there's nothing to convert directly. Use the OCR tool and choose Word as the output format instead.
    What's the maximum file size?
    Up to 100 MB per file.
