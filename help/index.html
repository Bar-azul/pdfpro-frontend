Help Center

Find answers to the most common questions about PDFPro.

How do I convert a PDF to Word? +

Click on "PDF to Word", upload your file, and click "Process File". Your converted document will be ready in seconds.

Is my data safe? +

Yes. All files are encrypted during transfer and automatically deleted from our servers after 1 hour. We never read your documents.

What is the file size limit? +

Free plan: 100MB per file. Pro plan: 500MB per file.

How many conversions can I do? +

Free plan: 5 per day. Pro plan: unlimited.

Does OCR support Hebrew? +

Yes! Our OCR engine supports Hebrew, Arabic, English and 40+ languages simultaneously.

Can I cancel my subscription? +

Yes, at any time from your account settings. No questions asked.
