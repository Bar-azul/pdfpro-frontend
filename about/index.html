About PDFPro

PDFPro is a set of free online PDF tools built with one specific gap in mind: most converters mangle Hebrew and other right-to-left languages.

Why it exists

Convert a Hebrew PDF with a typical online tool and you often get reversed words, flipped numbers and paragraphs in the wrong order. PDFPro is built and tested on Hebrew and mixed Hebrew-English documents first, and it works just as well for English-only files.

Who we are

PDFPro is an independent project from Israel, built for people who work with Hebrew documents every day and are tired of converters that reverse them. It is not affiliated with any other product called PDF Pro.

At a glance

  • 18 tools for PDFs, scans and recordings
  • 26 practical guides in English and Hebrew
  • 7 languages for document translation
  • 2026 the year the site launched

Who it's for

  • Renters and landlords: sign a contract without printing, and send an ID copy safely.
  • Freelancers and small businesses: quotes, invoices, bank statements to Excel and call transcripts.
  • Students: convert notes and slides, combine files and transcribe lectures.
  • Anyone filing paperwork: shrink files for government, bank and pension websites, and combine payslips into one file.
  • Travellers: translate menus and documents from abroad.

How your files are handled

Files are uploaded over an encrypted connection, processed on our server, and deleted automatically one hour later. We don't look at, keep or share their contents. Details are in the privacy policy.

What you can do here

  • Convert: PDF to Word, PDF to Excel, PDF to PowerPoint, PDF to JPG, and back with Word to PDF and JPG to PDF.
  • Organize: merge, split, compress and rotate.
  • Edit and secure: sign, watermark, redact sensitive details, add a password and remove one.
  • Text and language: OCR for scans, document translation and audio transcription.

How we test the tools

Every tool is tested on real Hebrew documents: contracts with numbers and dates, bank statements, payslips, phone scans and menus. When something doesn't work well in Hebrew, we fix it in the tool rather than hide it. PDF to Word, for example, rebuilds each Hebrew line in reading order, and the compressor returns your original file if it can't shrink it without damage. Tool pages and guides also say what a tool does not do, for instance that machine translation doesn't replace a certified translation.

Our principles

  • Really free: no sign-up, no watermark and no payment screen after the upload.
  • Transparent: each tool's limits are on the pricing page, and the outside services we use are named in the privacy policy.
  • Minimal data: no account needed, and files aren't kept.

How it's funded

The tools are free. The site shows ads that pay for the servers. A paid plan for advanced features, such as larger files and longer recordings, will come later; the basic tools will stay free.
